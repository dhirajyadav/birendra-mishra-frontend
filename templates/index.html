FAQ

Common questions

Practical details regarding consultation scheduling, confidential discussions, and advisory arrangements.

How far in advance can I book?

Appointments can be booked from tomorrow up to 60 days ahead. Availability respects working hours in Asia/Dubai and existing calendar commitments.

How do meetings take place?

Every confirmed booking includes a Google Meet link. You also receive a calendar invitation to the email address you provide.

What are the consultation fees?

Fees are listed on this page and confirmed during booking. Current amounts are placeholders until the practice owner sets final pricing. Discovery calls may be complimentary.

Can I reschedule or cancel?

Yes. Your confirmation email includes a secure link to reschedule or cancel. Please give as much notice as possible so the slot can be released.

Is the conversation confidential?

Yes. Engagements are handled with independence and confidentiality appropriate to board-level and executive discussions.

Do you offer ongoing advisory?

Monthly advisory retainers are available by enquiry. Use the contact form or select the retainer option when booking.
